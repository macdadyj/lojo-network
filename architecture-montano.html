Field architecture · Montano, NM

Private path for latch control. Cameras stay separate.

This is how remote cattle and wild-horse trap sites have used LOJO for years: the network carries control. Watching video is still a normal camera app.

  1. 1 · Edge site — Raspberry Pi with dual-relay hardware at the trap; cell+Wi‑Fi or Starlink as last mile.
  2. 2 · Join LOJO — The Pi connects to the managed private network (same idea as a phone with Connect).
  3. 3 · Control only — A Socket.IO device talks to the Pub-Node for GPIO / spring-latch open and close.
  4. 4 · Rancher phone — Connect on the phone, open the control page in a browser, toggle the latch.
  5. 5 · Cameras — Typical security-camera apps for viewing. Not routed through the Pub-Node.

Why it matters for buyers: LOJO is the managed private pipe. Specialty apps keep doing what they already do. You are not forced into one giant “everything” portal.
