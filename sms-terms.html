SMS program · last updated September 22, 2026

SMS Terms and Conditions

These terms cover the Lojo Engineering transactional SMS program. Messages are sent from (505) 207-4190.

Program description

Lojo Engineering sends transactional text messages about computer-systems and AI-hardware projects. Typical messages include project status, site-visit coordination, shipping and delivery updates, and replies when you text us a question. This program does not send marketing promotions.

How to opt in

Joining the text program is voluntary. You can request and purchase Lojo Engineering services without it. If you want texts, you can join in either of these ways:

  1. By text: Text START to (505) 207-4190. Keyword instructions are also posted at sms-opt-in.html.
  2. By web form: On Get Started or in the form below, check the SMS consent box yourself and enter a mobile number, then use Email my request with SMS opt-in. The box is not pre-checked and is not required. Email my request (no SMS) sends an email request only — it is not SMS consent, and it does not need a phone number.

Text START to (505) 207-4190

Up to 8 msgs/mo. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel. Reply HELP for help.

Web form

Use this form, or the same form on Get Started, to email team@lojo.engineering. Name and email are required for a reply. The SMS checkbox is optional, unchecked, and not required. A mobile number is required only when you choose the SMS button after checking the box. No server-side form processor is used; submit opens your mail app.

Add SMS updates (optional)

Skip this section to contact us by email only. Checking the box is a separate choice. It is not required to request service or to purchase. The box stays unchecked unless you check it.

SMS opt-in is off. You can submit with name and email only — no phone number and no consent checkbox.

If your browser does not open mail, send the same details to team@lojo.engineering. Say that you do not want SMS unless you checked the box and included a mobile number.

Frequency, rates, and support

  • Frequency: up to 8 messages per month
  • Message and data rates may apply
  • Support: team@lojo.engineering or text the program number
  • Business location: Albuquerque, New Mexico

Reply STOP to cancel. After you text STOP, we will send a one-time confirmation and stop program messages to that number.

Reply HELP for help. We will reply with support instructions and team@lojo.engineering.

Consent is not required to purchase

Consent to receive SMS is not required as a condition of purchasing any Lojo Engineering goods or services. You can buy Connect, Operate, or Build without joining this text program. You can also submit Get Started with name and email only, using Email my request (no SMS), and never provide a phone number.

Carrier disclaimer

Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Read the full Privacy Policy.
