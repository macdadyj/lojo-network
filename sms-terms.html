SMS program · last updated September 23, 2026

SMS Terms and Conditions

These terms cover the Lojo Engineering transactional SMS program. Messages are sent from (505) 207-4190.

Program description

Lojo Engineering sends transactional text messages about computer-systems and AI-hardware projects. Typical messages include project status, site-visit coordination, shipping and delivery updates, and replies when you text us a question. This program does not send marketing promotions.

How to opt in

Joining the text program is voluntary. You can request and purchase Lojo Engineering services without it. If you want texts, you can join in either of these ways:

  1. By text: Text START to (505) 207-4190. Keyword instructions are also posted at sms-opt-in.html.
  2. By web form: Open SMS signup. Users enter a mobile phone number and check the consent box. The box is unchecked until you check it. Name, email, and mobile phone are required on that form because it is only an SMS opt-in. Get Started is a service request by email. It does not ask for a phone number, and SMS consent is not required to submit it or to purchase.

Text START to (505) 207-4190

Up to 8 msgs/mo. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel. Reply HELP for help.

Web form

The public web opt-in form is sms-signup.html. That page always shows a Mobile phone field and an SMS consent checkbox. Users enter a mobile phone number and check the consent box. The box is unchecked until you check it. Submit sends only after the phone number is filled in and the box is checked. No server-side form processor is used; submit opens your mail app.

That form is not required to purchase. For a project request with no texts, use Get Started. Name and email are enough there, and that page does not ask for a phone number or SMS consent.

Frequency, rates, and support

  • Frequency: up to 8 messages per month
  • Message and data rates may apply
  • Support: team@lojo.engineering or text the program number
  • Business location: Albuquerque, New Mexico

Reply STOP to cancel. After you text STOP, we will send a one-time confirmation and stop program messages to that number.

Reply HELP for help. We will reply with support instructions and team@lojo.engineering.

Consent is not required to purchase

Consent to receive SMS is not required as a condition of purchasing any Lojo Engineering goods or services. You can buy Connect, Operate, or Build without joining this text program. You can also submit Get Started with name and email only and never provide a phone number.

Carrier disclaimer

Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
