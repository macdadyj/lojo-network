FAQ

Is this just Tailscale or a consumer VPN?

No. Consumer VPNs hide browsing. DIY mesh tools leave you as the admin — updates, devices, outages, and policy. LOJO is a managed private network: we design it, install the connectors, onboard your people, and support it. You get one bill and an operator, not a product to babysit.

What stays off the public internet?

Work systems on the private network — file shares, cameras, office PCs, and optional private cloud machines — are reached through the private path, not published as open public services. Your team still uses the normal internet for email and the web; the private network is for work assets you do not want exposed.

Is my network shared with other customers?

Your network is isolated from other customers. Until a fully separate account is turned on for you, it rides the LOJO operator network. That is normal for how we bootstrap and operate early accounts. We will tell you if that matters for your compliance or risk picture — calmly, before you buy, not after an incident.

Who supports outages?

LOJO does. Email team@lojo.engineering when something is down or a device will not connect. We own the network design and the connectors — you are not left googling VPN error codes at midnight.

When does private AI make sense?

After the network is stable and you have a concrete use — summarizing internal docs, drafting from private files, or keeping prompts off public chatbots. Until then, skip it. Private AI is optional: on-machine weights, Gemini in your GCP project, or hosted Grok when you do not need an air gap.
