Deployment modes

Where it runs. What leaves. What doesn’t.

Same product idea in every mode — a private control path — with an honest boundary about residency today vs what Build can place in your environment.

Today

LOJO-managed overlay

Where it runs
A LOJO-operated private overlay and control plane — you Connect; we run the path.
What leaves
Optional capture media and vision analysis may use LOJO-operated cloud services unless you choose a Build placement in your boundary.
What doesn’t
Latch & control stay on the private path after Connect — not on the open internet.

Build

Customer VPC / on-prem

Where it runs
Your VPC or on-prem stack — scoped and operated under a Build engagement.
What leaves
Only what you approve in that scope (and nothing we invent as “already productized”).
What doesn’t
Control placement you own; cameras and specialty apps can stay separate (Montano pattern).

Not a SKU

Full air-gap

Where it runs
Isolated environments are possible as engineered work — not a turnkey LOJO product today.
What leaves
Defined per engagement; we won’t market air-gap as already shipped.
What doesn’t
Don’t read “private path” as “air-gapped out of the box.”

Honesty line

Today: On the managed path, optional capture media and vision analysis may use LOJO-operated cloud services — not your VPC by default. Control (Connect → your sites → actuators) stays on the private path. Customer-owned cloud, VPC, or on-prem resources are a Build path when you want residency inside your boundary.
