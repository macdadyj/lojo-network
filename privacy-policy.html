Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Lojo Engineering (“LOJO,” “we,” “us”) collects and uses information, including mobile numbers used for transactional project SMS from (505) 207-4190.

Who we are

Lojo Engineering is based in Albuquerque, New Mexico. We design and operate computer systems and AI-hardware projects. Contact: team@lojo.engineering.

Information we collect

When you email a project request, we collect the name and email you send. A mobile number is collected only if you choose to provide one. We record SMS consent only when you opt in.

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Mobile phone number, only if you opt in to SMS or include it in a message you send us
  • Project or site details you choose to send us
  • SMS opt-in method (optional web-form checkbox or text keyword) and the date of consent, when you opt in
  • Messages you send to (505) 207-4190, including STOP and HELP requests

How we use information

We use your name and email to reply to project requests. SMS is optional and is not required to request service or to purchase. If you opt in, we use your mobile number to deliver transactional SMS about computer-systems and AI-hardware projects: project status, site-visit coordination, shipping and delivery updates, and replies to questions you send us. We also use contact details to honor opt-out requests and to answer support questions.

Message frequency is up to 8 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply.

Mobile numbers are not sold or shared for marketing

Lojo Engineering does not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers or other mobile opt-in information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

We may use messaging infrastructure providers solely to deliver the messages you requested. Those providers may process your number only to transmit the SMS you opted into — not to market their own products to you.

SMS program details

  • Program: Lojo Engineering transactional project SMS
  • Number: (505) 207-4190
  • Frequency: up to 8 msgs/mo
  • Message and data rates may apply
  • Reply STOP to cancel. You will receive a one-time confirmation and we will stop sending program messages.
  • Reply HELP for help. You will receive a help message with this contact information.
  • You can also email team@lojo.engineering to opt out or ask questions.

See SMS Terms, how to opt in by text, and SMS signup for the web form. On that page, users enter a mobile phone number and check the consent box. The box is unchecked until they check it. Get Started is a service request and does not require a phone number or SMS consent.

Other website data

This site is static. We do not run an account system or advertising pixels on these pages. If you email us, your message is processed by your email provider and ours. If you use a mailto: form, your device’s mail app creates the message; we receive only what you send.

Retention and your choices

We keep mobile numbers and consent records while you are opted in and as needed to honor STOP requests and legal obligations. Email team@lojo.engineering to request deletion of contact records we control, or text STOP to (505) 207-4190 to leave the SMS program.
